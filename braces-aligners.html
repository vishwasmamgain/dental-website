What Are Braces and Aligners?

Braces and aligners are orthodontic treatments that gradually move teeth into proper alignment. They address issues like crowding, gaps, overbites, underbites, and crossbites. At Bhuvneshwari Dental Clinic in Haridwar, Dr. Shivangi Sharma provides both traditional metal braces and clear aligner options for patients aged 12 and above.

Depending on the severity of misalignment, treatment can take anywhere from 6 months to 2 years. Clear aligners are popular among adults and teens who prefer a less visible option, while traditional braces remain effective for complex cases.

Types of Orthodontic Treatment We Offer

  • Metal Braces: Traditional brackets and wires, effective for all types of misalignment. Most affordable option.
  • Clear Ceramic Braces: Tooth-colored brackets that are less noticeable than metal braces.
  • Clear Aligners: Removable, nearly invisible trays that gradually shift teeth. Ideal for mild to moderate cases.
  • Self-Ligating Braces: Modern braces with a sliding mechanism that may reduce treatment time and discomfort.

Who Can Benefit from Braces or Aligners?

Orthodontic treatment is suitable for both teenagers and adults. Common concerns that can be addressed include:

  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Overbite or underbite
  • Crossbite
  • Open bite
  • Misaligned dental arches

Additionally, properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease. Straight teeth also improve chewing function and may enhance speech.

What to Expect During Treatment

The orthodontic journey at our clinic in Haridwar typically involves:

  1. Initial Consultation: Dr. Sharma examines your teeth, takes X-rays if needed, and discusses treatment options.
  2. Treatment Planning: A customized plan is created based on your specific needs and goals.
  3. Placement: Braces are fitted, or clear aligners are provided with detailed instructions.
  4. Regular Adjustments: Follow-up visits every 4-8 weeks to monitor progress and make adjustments.
  5. Retention: After treatment, retainers are prescribed to maintain your new smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Braces & Aligners

At what age can my child start braces?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends children have their first orthodontic checkup by age 7. However, treatment typically begins between ages 9-14 when most permanent teeth have erupted. Adults can also benefit from orthodontic treatment at any age.

Are clear aligners as effective as traditional braces?

Clear aligners can effectively treat mild to moderate alignment issues. For complex cases involving severe crowding or bite problems, traditional braces may be more effective. Dr. Sharma will assess your specific situation and recommend the best option for your goals.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment duration varies by case complexity. Mild cases may take 6-12 months, while moderate to severe cases can require 18-24 months. Regular follow-up visits are essential to ensure treatment stays on track.
