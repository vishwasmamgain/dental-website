When to Seek Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies can be painful and stressful. At Bhuvneshwari Dental Clinic in Haridwar, we understand that dental issues don't always happen during regular office hours. That's why Dr. Shivangi Sharma offers emergency dental services 7 days a week.

Seek immediate care if you experience:

  • Severe tooth pain: Persistent, throbbing pain that doesn't go away with over-the-counter pain medication
  • Swelling: Swelling in the face, jaw, or gums that may indicate infection
  • Broken or chipped teeth: Especially if accompanied by pain or bleeding
  • Lost filling or crown: Which can expose sensitive tooth structures
  • Dental trauma: Injury to the mouth or teeth from an accident
  • Abscess: A pus-filled infection that can spread to other parts of the body

What to Do Before You Arrive

If you're experiencing a dental emergency:

  • Call our clinic immediately at +91 97608 67857 or WhatsApp us
  • Rinse your mouth with warm salt water
  • Apply a cold compress to reduce swelling
  • Take over-the-counter pain medication if needed
  • Save any broken tooth pieces in milk or saliva
  • Avoid chewing on the affected side

Note: If you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, or if bleeding is severe and won't stop, go to the nearest hospital emergency room immediately.
