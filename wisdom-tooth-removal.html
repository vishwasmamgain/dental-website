Understanding Wisdom Teeth

Wisdom teeth, also known as third molars, are the last teeth to emerge, typically appearing between ages 17 and 25. They are located at the very back of your mouth, and many people don't have enough space for them to come in properly. This can lead to impaction, pain, infection, and damage to adjacent teeth.

At Bhuvneshwari Dental Clinic in Haridwar, Dr. Shivangi Sharma recommends wisdom tooth extraction when these teeth are causing problems or are likely to cause issues in the future. Early removal can prevent complications and preserve the health of your other teeth.

Signs You May Need Wisdom Tooth Removal

  • Pain or discomfort at the back of the mouth
  • Swelling around the jaw area
  • Difficulty opening your mouth wide
  • Red, swollen, or bleeding gums
  • Bad breath or unpleasant taste
  • Headaches or jaw pain
  • Evidence of decay or gum disease near the wisdom tooth

Even if you don't have symptoms, regular dental checkups with X-rays can help Dr. Sharma monitor your wisdom teeth and recommend removal if they're likely to cause problems.

The Extraction Process

Wisdom tooth removal at our clinic is performed with your comfort and safety as the top priority. The procedure typically involves:

  1. Consultation and X-rays: Dr. Sharma examines your mouth and takes panoramic X-rays to assess the position and angle of your wisdom teeth.
  2. Anesthesia: Local anesthesia is administered to numb the area. For anxious patients, sedation options may be discussed.
  3. Extraction: The tooth is carefully removed. Simple extractions involve loosening and removing the tooth, while surgical extractions may require small incisions in the gum or removal of bone around the tooth.
  4. Recovery: Stitching may be needed if surgical extraction was performed. Dr. Sharma provides detailed aftercare instructions to ensure proper healing.

Recovery and Aftercare

Most patients recover within 3-7 days. To promote healing:

  • Apply ice packs to reduce swelling
  • Eat soft foods for the first few days
  • Avoid strenuous activity for 24-48 hours
  • Don't smoke or use straws (this can dislodge the blood clot)
  • Keep the extraction site clean with gentle rinsing
  • Take prescribed pain medication or over-the-counter pain relievers as directed

If you experience severe pain, excessive bleeding, fever, or pus discharge, contact our clinic immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Wisdom Tooth Removal

Do all wisdom teeth need to be removed?

No, not all wisdom teeth require extraction. If they are fully erupted, properly aligned, and can be cleaned effectively, they may be left in place. However, many people lack sufficient space, leading to impaction or other problems. Dr. Sharma will assess your specific situation and recommend the best course of action.

Is wisdom tooth removal painful?

The procedure itself is performed under local anesthesia, so you shouldn't feel pain during the extraction. After the anesthesia wears off, you may experience some discomfort and swelling, which can be managed with prescribed or over-the-counter pain medication. Most patients report that the recovery is easier than expected.

What is the best age for wisdom tooth removal?

While wisdom teeth can be removed at any age, the optimal time is typically between 17-21 years old. At this age, the tooth roots are not fully developed, and the bone is less dense, making extraction easier and recovery faster. However, adults can also successfully undergo wisdom tooth removal.
